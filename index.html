RESEARCH FOCUS / 研究关注

Generative AI ×
Digital Cultural Heritage

生成式人工智能 × 数字文化遗产

My research explores the intersection of generative artificial intelligence and digital cultural heritage, examining how cultural knowledge can be represented, experienced, and critically interpreted through generative technologies, interaction design, and immersive media.

It brings together intangible cultural heritage practices, historical archives, and emerging interactive environments, with particular attention to embodied participation, aesthetic experience, cultural understanding, and heritage governance.

本研究关注生成式人工智能与数字文化遗产的交叉领域，探索文化知识如何通过生成式技术、交互设计与沉浸式媒介得到表达、体验和批判性理解。

研究涵盖非物质文化遗产实践、历史档案及新兴交互环境，重点关注具身参与、美学体验、文化理解与遗产治理之间的关系。

RESEARCH CONTEXT / 研究情境

From Local Practice to Broader Waterscapes

从地方实践走向更广阔的水域文化景观

My previous work in ZuoTan Village provided practical experience in exploring local knowledge, spatial experience, and the digital translation of cultural elements.

Building on this foundation, my emerging research extends beyond a single village to the broader waterscapes and living environments associated with Tanka communities across the Pearl River Delta.

This expanded cultural geography offers opportunities to examine waterways, settlements, everyday practices, historical archives, and embodied experiences together. Related cultural contexts will be progressively incorporated as the research develops.

此前围绕左滩村开展的实践与研究，为我探索地方知识、空间经验及文化元素的数字媒介转译提供了初步基础。

在此基础上，未来研究将不再局限于单一村落个案，而是进一步扩展至珠江三角洲更广泛的疍民水域文化与生活环境。

这一扩展使水域空间、聚居形态、日常实践、历史档案与具身经验能够被置于相互关联的文化地理语境中理解。其他相关文化语境也将在研究推进过程中逐步纳入。

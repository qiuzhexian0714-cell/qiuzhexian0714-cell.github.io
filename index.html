03 / RESEARCH DIRECTIONS研究方向

Beyond symbols.
Tracing cultural connections.

超越符号，追溯文化关联。

RESEARCH FOCUS / 研究关注

Relational Knowledge Representation and Interactive Design for AI-Mediated Digital Cultural Heritage

生成式人工智能介入下数字文化遗产的关系性知识表征与交互设计研究

My research explores how cultural knowledge can be connected, interpreted, and experienced through generative AI and interactive design.

Rather than treating cultural heritage as a collection of visual symbols or a single story, I want to explore the relationships between historical sources, local practices, people, and places, while keeping different cultural interpretations open.

我的研究关注文化知识如何通过生成式人工智能与交互设计被关联、解释和体验。

我希望不再仅仅将文化遗产视为视觉符号的集合或单一的故事，而是探索历史资料、地方实践、人与空间之间的关系，同时保留不同文化解释的开放性。

RESEARCH FRAMEWORK 研究框架

KEY RESEARCH QUESTIONS / 核心研究问题

Key research questions

  1. RQ1

    Relational Knowledge Representation

    关系性知识表征

    How can historical records, oral memories, and local practices be organised into traceable relational knowledge while preserving their sources, contexts, and different interpretations?

    来自历史档案、口述记忆与地方实践的文化信息，如何在保留资料来源、具体语境和不同解释的前提下，被组织为可追溯的关系性知识？

  2. RQ2

    Generative AI & Interactive Exploration

    生成式 AI 与交互探索

    How can generative AI and interaction design help people trace cultural evidence, compare different interpretations, and actively explore heritage knowledge rather than simply receive generated content?

    如何将关系性知识与生成式 AI、交互设计相结合，让参与者能够主动追溯文化依据、比较不同解释，而不只是被动接受系统生成的内容？

  3. RQ3

    Evaluation & Cultural Responsibility

    研究评价与文化责任

    How can we evaluate the reliability of generated knowledge, participants' cultural understanding and agency, and communities' involvement in interpreting and revising cultural representations?

    如何分别评价生成式交互的知识可信度、参与者的文化理解与能动性，以及相关社区参与文化解释和修订的权利？

RESEARCH CONTEXT / 研究情境

From Local Practice to Broader Waterscapes

从地方实践走向更广阔的水域文化景观

My previous fieldwork in Zuotan Village provided a starting point for exploring the relationships between local culture, everyday practices, and water-based environments.

The Tanka-related waterscapes of the Pearl River Delta offer one possible context for further research, bringing together historical records, living practices, community memories, and different cultural perspectives. However, this is a potential research setting rather than a fixed boundary for the project.

此前在顺德左滩村开展的田野实践，为我研究地方文化、日常生活实践与水域环境之间的关系提供了起点。

珠江三角洲疍民相关的水域文化遗产是未来可能进一步研究的情境之一，其中涉及历史资料、活态实践、社区记忆与不同的文化解释。但它只是潜在的研究入口，而不是整个博士研究的固定边界。

RESEARCH PATH / 五阶段研究路径

Research path

Research Stage研究阶段Proposed Work可以开展的工作
01Cultural Knowledge Research文化知识研究

Select a specific heritage context and examine historical records, oral accounts, and local practices to understand the sources, contexts, and different interpretations of cultural knowledge.

选取具体遗产主题，结合历史文献、口述材料与地方实践，梳理文化知识的来源、语境及不同主体的解释。

02Relational Knowledge Construction关系性知识建构

Identify relationships between people, places, events, objects, and cultural practices. Document evidence sources, differences in interpretation, and uncertainties to build a traceable and revisable knowledge structure.

识别人物、地点、事件、物件与文化实践之间的关系，记录证据来源、解释差异与不确定性，形成可追溯、可修订的知识结构。

03Generative Interactive Design生成式交互设计

Integrate the knowledge structure into generative systems and develop interactive prototypes that support knowledge retrieval, evidence tracing, comparison of different interpretations, and active exploration.

将文化知识结构引入生成式系统，设计支持知识检索、证据追溯、多元解释比较与主动探索的交互原型。

04Research Evaluation研究评价

Use prototype comparisons, participant testing, and expert reviews to examine cultural understanding, participant agency, interactive experience, and the reliability of cultural knowledge.

通过原型比较、参与者测试与专家评议，分别考察文化理解、参与者能动性、交互体验及文化知识的可信度。

05Governance & Design Methods治理与设计方法

Explore cultural data permissions, community feedback, and content revision processes. Identify the conditions, limitations, and transferable design principles of the proposed methods.

探索文化资料授权、社区反馈与内容修订机制，总结方法的适用条件、局限及可迁移的设计原则。

RESEARCH OUTLOOK / 研究展望

From situated knowledge to transferable design methods.

从具体情境中的文化知识，走向可迁移的设计方法。

By developing and evaluating interactive prototypes in specific heritage contexts, I hope to build design methods that support reliable cultural knowledge, multiple interpretations, and meaningful participation.

我希望通过具体文化遗产情境中的交互原型与研究评价，逐步形成兼顾文化知识可信度、多元解释和有意义参与的设计方法。
